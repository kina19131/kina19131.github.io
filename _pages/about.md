Hi, I'm Kina — I build machine learning for database systems on the Db2 Query Optimization team at [IBM](https://www.ibm.com/ca-en).

Our team develops the [Db2 AI Query Optimizer](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/db2/12.1.x?topic=optimization-ai-query-optimizer), which brings neural networks into Db2's cost-based optimizer to learn data distributions and produce better cardinality estimates. My work sits on both the research and engineering sides. I'm running an empirical study of how to represent and search large spaces of equivalent query transformations, comparing structured representations, learned embeddings, and Bayesian optimization. I'm also building ML training infrastructure inside Db2, so models are trained and deployed right next to the engine that serves enterprises like RBC, Bank of America, and Loblaws.

Before this, I built backend infrastructure for [Db2 on Cloud](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/db2-as-a-service): designing APIs, shipping cloud database features, and hardening the service across AWS and Azure. Along the way I used generative models (Graph VAEs, GANs) to stress-test those systems, generating synthetic SQL workloads that surfaced query optimizer bugs traditional test suites missed. That work is what first drew me to optimization.

Beyond IBM, I led a fairness-aware adversarial debiasing system for AI-driven hiring at the [AI4Good Mila Lab](https://www.ai4goodlab.com/), and I've published at IJCNLP-AACL, and ICCC — on topics ranging from explainability in agentic AI to NLP-based humor detection.

I'm always interested in pushing boundaries and exploring new things, which is what draws me to research — I've been fortunate to pursue this with [Prof. Michael Guerzhoy](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~guerzhoy/), [Dr. Shaina Raza](https://scholar.google.ca/citations?user=chcz7RMAAAAJ&hl=ja), and [Prof. Linyi Li](https://www.sfu.ca/fas/computing/people/faculty/faculty-members/linyi-li.html).

I studied [Engineering Science](https://engsci.utoronto.ca/program/what-is-engsci/) at the University of Toronto, majoring in Machine Learning with a Robotics minor.
