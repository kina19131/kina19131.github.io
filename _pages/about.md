Hi, I'm Kina — I build machine learning for database systems on the Db2 Query Optimization team at [IBM](https://www.ibm.com/ca-en).

On the [Db2 AI Query Optimizer](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/db2/12.1.x?topic=optimization-ai-query-optimizer), I research how to search large spaces of equivalent query rewrites using learned embeddings and Bayesian optimization, and build the infrastructure to train and deploy models inside the database engine itself.

Before this, I worked on [Db2 on Cloud](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/db2-as-a-service), where I used generative models (GANs, Graph VAEs) to create synthetic SQL workloads that caught optimizer bugs traditional tests missed. That's what pulled me toward optimization.

Outside IBM, I've worked on fairness in AI hiring at the [AI4Good Mila Lab](https://www.ai4goodlab.com/), explainability in agentic AI, and NLP, with publications at IJCNLP-AACL and ICCC. I've been fortunate to do research with [Prof. Michael Guerzhoy](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~guerzhoy/), [Dr. Shaina Raza](https://scholar.google.ca/citations?user=chcz7RMAAAAJ&hl=ja), and [Prof. Linyi Li](https://www.sfu.ca/fas/computing/people/faculty/faculty-members/linyi-li.html).

I studied [Engineering Science](https://engsci.utoronto.ca/program/what-is-engsci/) (Machine Learning major, Robotics minor) at the University of Toronto.
